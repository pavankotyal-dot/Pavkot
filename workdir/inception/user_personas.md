# User Personas - PremiumCard Loyalty & Rewards Program

## Customer Personas

### 1. Premium Lifestyle Consumer - "Alexandra"
**Demographics:** 35-50 years old, household income $150K+, urban professional
**Spending Behavior:** High-value purchases, luxury goods, premium services
**Motivations:** Exclusive experiences, status recognition, premium benefits
**Pain Points:** Generic rewards, lack of personalization, limited luxury options
**Program Goals:** Access to exclusive experiences, premium tier benefits, luxury redemptions

### 2. Travel Enthusiast - "Marcus"
**Demographics:** 28-45 years old, frequent business/leisure traveler
**Spending Behavior:** Airlines, hotels, travel services, dining
**Motivations:** Travel rewards, airline miles, hotel points, travel convenience
**Pain Points:** Complex redemption processes, limited travel options, blackout dates
**Program Goals:** Maximize travel rewards, flexible booking options, travel-related benefits

### 3. Everyday Spender - "Sarah"
**Demographics:** 25-40 years old, family-oriented, budget-conscious
**Spending Behavior:** Groceries, gas, dining, everyday purchases
**Motivations:** Cashback rewards, practical benefits, value optimization
**Pain Points:** Low reward rates, complicated programs, limited cashback options
**Program Goals:** Maximize everyday spending rewards, simple redemption, practical benefits

### 4. Business Professional - "David"
**Demographics:** 30-55 years old, corporate executive, high business spending
**Spending Behavior:** Business expenses, entertainment, corporate travel
**Motivations:** Business benefits, expense management, corporate rewards
**Pain Points:** Complex expense reporting, limited business-focused rewards
**Program Goals:** Business expense optimization, corporate benefits, streamlined reporting

## Administrative Personas

### 5. General Manager - "Jennifer"
**Role:** Senior executive overseeing loyalty program strategy
**Responsibilities:** Program configuration, strategic decisions, performance monitoring
**Goals:** Drive business objectives, optimize program ROI, ensure customer satisfaction
**Challenges:** Balancing costs with benefits, measuring program effectiveness
**Key Metrics:** Customer acquisition, retention, CLV, program profitability

### 6. Sales Representative - "Michael"
**Role:** Customer-facing support representative
**Responsibilities:** Customer support, issue resolution, account management
**Goals:** Resolve customer issues, maintain satisfaction, support sales objectives
**Challenges:** Limited system access, complex customer requests, escalation procedures
**Key Metrics:** Customer satisfaction, issue resolution time, support quality
